Hash Commitment Dice Guess

Commit a number. Opponent gets 3 guesses. Take turns.

  1. You pick 1–6 and a salt, then send the hash.
  2. Opponent gets 3 guesses.
  3. After 3 guesses, reveal your number + salt.
  4. If they guessed it, they win this round. Then switch roles.

Keep this secret until after the 3 guesses. Make it long and random.
